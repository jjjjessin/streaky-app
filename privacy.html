Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026 · Version 1.3.1

This policy explains how Streakie ("the app"), published by The Product People, handles information when you use it to track your habits.

In short: your goals and check-ins stay on your device and we cannot see them. There is no account and no ads inside the app. What leaves the phone is anonymous statistics about which features are used and, to measure our own ads, whether you installed and opened the app. Your goals are never part of it, and you can switch both off in the app.

1. Who is responsible

Streakie is published by The Product People. For any privacy question, contact hello@theproductpeople.se.

2. What the app stores, and where

Streakie stores your habits, check-ins, pauses and settings in a file on your device. The Home Screen widget reads the same file through a shared container on the device, so it can show and update your habits. None of this is sent to us or anyone else.

  • no user accounts and no sign-in,
  • no servers and no cloud sync holding your habits,
  • no ads inside the app, and the advertising identifier (IDFA) is only used if you allow tracking in Apple's prompt (see 2b).

2a. Anonymous usage statistics

To see which parts of Streakie are useful, the app sends anonymous usage statistics: which screens and features are used (for example "a goal was created", "a check-in was made", "a pause was started"), with coarse details such as the type of goal (daily, weekly, avoid), streak length or number of goals, plus app version, iOS version and device type.

Never sent: goal names or anything you type, your check-in history, your name, email, location, contacts or the advertising identifier. The statistics use a random identifier created by the app; it is not linked to you, your Apple ID or any other app, and no personal profile is created.

Who receives it: the statistics are processed on our behalf by PostHog on servers in the EU and used only to improve Streakie. They are not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Rather not? Turn off Share anonymous usage statistics under Settings → Privacy and the app stops sending immediately.

2b. Measuring our ads (Meta)

We advertise Streakie on Instagram and Facebook. To know whether those ads work, the app includes Meta's SDK, which tells Meta Platforms that the app was installed and opened, together with technical details such as app version, iOS version and device type.

Never sent: your goals, check-ins, names you enter or anything else you do in the app. We do not use Facebook Login and send no other events.

Tracking prompt: after onboarding, iOS asks whether Streakie may track you. Only if you tap Allow is the advertising identifier (IDFA) included, so Meta can match the install to an ad you saw. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, Meta only receives aggregated, privacy-preserving install counts through Apple's SKAdNetwork. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Rather not at all? Turning off Share anonymous usage statistics under Settings → Privacy also stops these Meta events. Meta processes the data as described in its own privacy policy, which may involve transfers outside the EU under Meta's safeguards. The legal basis is your consent (tracking) and our legitimate interest in measuring our advertising (install counts).

If you use iCloud or iTunes device backups, Apple may include the app's data in your backup under Apple's own privacy terms.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Streakie schedules reminders locally on your device. No push server is involved and no notification content leaves the phone. You can change or turn off reminders in the app's settings or in iOS Settings.

4. Sharing

When you tap Share, Streakie creates an image of your streak and opens the iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose where to send it, and what happens afterwards is governed by the app you share to.

4b. Doing a goal with friends

If you challenge a friend or do a goal together, Streakie shares that one goal through Apple iCloud (CloudKit sharing) in your own iCloud account. The shared goal, the first name you enter and your daily check-ins for that goal are visible to the friends you invite, and theirs to you. Your other goals and data are never shared. We run no server for this and cannot read the shared data; Apple stores it under its own privacy terms. Leaving or ending a shared goal stops sharing; your history in it stays visible to the others as part of that shared goal.

5. Children

Streakie is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete any habit inside the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
