Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026 · Version 1.1

This policy explains how Streaky ("the app"), published by The Product People, handles information when you use it to track your habits.

In short: your goals and check-ins stay on your device and we cannot see them. There is no account, no ads and no tracking across apps. The only thing that leaves the phone is anonymous statistics about which features are used, and you can switch that off in the app.

1. Who is responsible

Streaky is published by The Product People. For any privacy question, contact hello@theproductpeople.se.

2. What the app stores, and where

Streaky stores your habits, check-ins, pauses and settings in a file on your device. The Home Screen widget reads the same file through a shared container on the device, so it can show and update your habits. None of this is sent to us or anyone else.

  • no user accounts and no sign-in,
  • no servers and no cloud sync holding your habits,
  • no advertising SDKs, no advertising identifier (IDFA) and no tracking across apps or websites.

2a. Anonymous usage statistics

To see which parts of Streaky are useful, the app sends anonymous usage statistics: which screens and features are used (for example "a goal was created", "a check-in was made", "a pause was started"), with coarse details such as the type of goal (daily, weekly, avoid), streak length or number of goals, plus app version, iOS version and device type.

Never sent: goal names or anything you type, your check-in history, your name, email, location, contacts or the advertising identifier. The statistics use a random identifier created by the app; it is not linked to you, your Apple ID or any other app, and no personal profile is created.

Who receives it: the statistics are processed on our behalf by PostHog on servers in the EU and used only to improve Streaky. They are not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Rather not? Turn off Share anonymous usage statistics under Settings → Privacy and the app stops sending immediately.

If you use iCloud or iTunes device backups, Apple may include the app's data in your backup under Apple's own privacy terms.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Streaky schedules reminders locally on your device. No push server is involved and no notification content leaves the phone. You can change or turn off reminders in the app's settings or in iOS Settings.

4. Sharing

When you tap Share, Streaky creates an image of your streak and opens the iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose where to send it, and what happens afterwards is governed by the app you share to.

5. Children

Streaky does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete any habit inside the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
