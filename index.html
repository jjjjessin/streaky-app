Features

Your streak, as a journey.

Challenge a friend. Or do it together.

Pick any goal, with any setting, and send a link. You both start at 0 on the day your friend joins, and their flame stands on your river so you always see who's ahead.

  • Challenge: the longest streak wins, 7 or 30 days or no end
  • Together: every check-in adds a plank to your weekly bridge
  • Send cheers: Keep it lit, Nice one, Proud of you
  • When it ends, keep going together or carry on alone. Your streak is never reset

Syncs through iCloud. Still no Streakie account.

Every day is a stepping stone

A winding river with a mark for each goal you completed. Your flame stands on today and jumps when you check in.

  • Open a chest at 7 days
  • Trophies at 30, 50, 75 and 100
  • Perfect days glow gold
  • Zoom out to see how far you've come

Prefer a simple list? Turn Quest off in Settings.

Life happens. Streaks survive.

Freeze a goal for today and it still counts as a streak day. Vacation, a sick day or a break? Pause it and your streak waits for you.

  • Earn a freeze at every level, freeze today or let it save a missed day
  • Changed your mind? Undo it
  • Mark a sick day and rest up; pauses end by themselves

Share your streak

Story-sized cards for your journey or a goal: your streak, your days since you started, or how close the next trophy is.

  • Swipe between four card styles
  • Add your own caption

Every day at a glance

Each goal has a calendar with done, missed, frozen, sick and paused days, plus your current and best streak.

  • Past days are locked to keep it honest
  • Edits you earn let you fix one

Your habits stay on your phone

No account and no ads in the app. Everything you enter is stored on your iPhone. Goals you share with friends sync through your own iCloud, and friends only see that goal, your first name and your check-ins. Only anonymous usage statistics and, to measure our own ads, whether the app was installed are sent. You can switch both off, and you decide in Apple's prompt whether tracking is allowed.

FAQ

Good questions.

Wasn't this app called Streaky?

Yes! Streaky is now Streakie. Same app, same flames, and your streaks carry over. Nothing to do on your side.

Is Streakie free?

Yes. Streakie is free to download and use, with no ads and no account.

How do I check in from the Home Screen?

Long-press your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Streakie and add the small or medium widget. Tap a circle in the widget to check in.

How do reminders work?

Streakie sends one evening check-in that lists everything still open, with a Mark all done button in the notification. Goals you only know the next day, like bedtime, get a morning check-in instead. Change the times in Settings → Notifications.

I forgot to check in yesterday.

Open Streakie and use the Log yesterday? card at the top. For older days, open the goal, tap the day in its calendar and spend an edit.

How do challenges work?

Open a goal and tap the friends button, or tap Start a challenge on My goals. Choose Challenge (longest streak wins) or Together (count it as a team), then send the link. Day 1 is the day your friend joins, and you both start at 0. Your friend gets the same goal with the same setting.

Does my friend need an account?

No Streakie account, but both of you need Streakie on an iPhone signed in to iCloud. Shared goals sync through iCloud, and friends only see that goal, your first name and your check-ins.

What happens when a challenge ends?

You see who won, then choose: keep going together with no end date, place the medal on your journey, or start a rematch. Whatever you pick, your goal and its streak carry on.

What is Quest?

A second view that shows your streak as a path of days along a river. Milestones give you edits and streak freezes. You can turn it off in Settings → Quest.

How does pausing work?

Long-press a goal, or open it and tap Pause this goal. Choose vacation, sick or a break and how long it lasts; the pause ends by itself. Paused days never break a streak.

Will I lose my streaks if I delete the app?

Yes. Your data lives only on your device, so deleting the app deletes your habits too.
