Quest

Your streak, as a journey.

Every day is a stepping stone on a winding river, with a mark for each goal you completed. Your flame stands on today and jumps when you check in.

  • Open a chest at 7 days, collect trophies at 30, 50, 75 and 100
  • Earn edits to fix a past day, and freezes that save a missed one
  • See day and night, and zoom out to see how far you've come

Prefer a simple list? Quest can be turned off in Settings.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is Streaky free?

Yes. Streaky is free to download and use, with no ads and no account.

How do I check in from the Home Screen?

Long-press your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Streaky and add the small or medium widget. Tap a circle in the widget to check in.

How do reminders work?

Streaky sends one evening check-in that lists everything still open, with a Mark all done button in the notification. Goals you only know the next day, like bedtime, get a morning check-in instead. Change the times in Settings → Notifications.

I forgot to check in yesterday.

Open Streaky and use the Log yesterday? card at the top. For older days, open the goal, tap the day in its calendar and spend an edit.

What is Quest?

A second view that shows your streak as a path of days along a river. Milestones give you edits and streak freezes. You can turn it off in Settings → Quest.

How does pausing work?

Long-press a goal, or open it and tap Pause this goal. Choose vacation, sick or a break and how long it lasts; the pause ends by itself. Paused days never break a streak.

Will I lose my streaks if I delete the app?

Yes. Your data lives only on your device, so deleting the app deletes your habits too.
